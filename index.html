Morse Code Table

Morse format: dot . and dash -. A space separates letters and / separates words. The symbols · and – are also recognized when decoding.

ASCII Formats

Decimal: 72 105 · Hexadecimal: 48 69 · Binary: 01001000 01101001 · Octal: 110 151. Separate each character with a space, comma, or new line. Binary without spaces (multiples of 8 digits) is also accepted.
